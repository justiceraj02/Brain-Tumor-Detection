📋 Detectable Tumor Types

What Can We Detect?

🔴

Glioma

Arises from glial cells in the brain. The most common and aggressive primary brain tumor type, requiring prompt medical attention.

🟡

Meningioma

Grows from the meninges — membranes surrounding the brain and spinal cord. Often benign and slow-growing, but may require monitoring.

🟢

Pituitary Tumor

Forms on the pituitary gland at the base of the brain. Usually slow-growing and benign, but can affect hormone production.

⚡ Key Symptoms

Common Warning Signs

  • Persistent headaches
  • Seizures or convulsions
  • Difficulty thinking or speaking
  • Personality or behavior changes
  • Weakness or numbness
  • Loss of balance or dizziness
  • Hearing or vision changes
  • Nausea or vomiting
